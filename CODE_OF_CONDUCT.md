# Code of Conduct

AuthZLab welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, assume good faith, and
focus discussion on the work and its technical merits.

Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing someone else's
private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or
restrict participation when needed to keep the project welcoming and safe.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact methods on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/stefanicla). For security vulnerabilities,
use the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a
public issue.
